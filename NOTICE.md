# Third-party material and data

Original public datasets are not bundled. Obtain them from their providers and
comply with their applicable terms. No registration credentials, download
tokens, private institutional observations or generated observations are included.

The unchanged Oxford RobotCar SDK reference excerpts in
`artifact/iclr-2027-composition/oxford-audit-2026-09-10/reference/` retain their
original attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 notices. Anonymity does not require
removing third-party authors' names or their notices. The manuscript and
publication templates are not included in this repository.

LIBVISO2, the KITTI native devkit and original dataset files are acquired
separately. Their absence and exact historical input identities are documented
in `artifact/KITTI_EXTERNAL_INPUTS.json` and `artifact/KITTI_REPRODUCTION.md`.

This artifact preparation does not assign a new repository-wide software
license or change the rights associated with any included work. Retain existing
notices when making a reproduction copy.
