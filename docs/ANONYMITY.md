# Anonymous export boundary

The repository is an independent export, not a clone of the development
repository. No parent Git history, private planning index, account configuration,
environment file, authentication material or unrelated paper is distributed.
Scientific code, checkpoint bytes and measured records are unchanged.

Two historical omission cases are explicit:

1. `artifact/ANONYMITY_OMISSIONS.json` retains the original SHA-256 and rationale
   for the identifying Oxford `stage_downloads.py` acquisition helper. It is not
   imported by supporting models or the read-only matrix audit. The historical
   full-source admission check remains fail-closed; the scoped wrapper reports
   the omission rather than silently authenticating absent bytes.
2. The private maintainer-correspondence draft formerly at
   `experiments/032_oxford_process_investigation/ins_multidate_check/maintainer_questions.md`
   is not part of this export. Its SHA-256 is
   `6d201aa63c49d8566716a4b2217390d0f0ed9f3d4d04f003d0b54185e6f1bfcf`.
   It is not a source, input, model, result or dependency of a scientific audit.
   Historical prose mentioning that draft remains historical; its underlying
   numerical diagnostics and their implementations are included.

Obsolete packaging/editorial receipts were replaced with the current inventory
and documentation. Original third-party citations, attribution and notices are
preserved. The manuscript, its LaTeX sources, bibliography, figure assets and
publication templates are excluded. The paper is submitted separately. The
export was scanned for known author/institution/path identifiers and common
credential patterns, including decompressed Torch checkpoint members.

The manifest verifies local file integrity, not anonymity of a future hosting
account. A publisher must also check account profiles, commit authors, remote
URLs, website analytics and issue/discussion content before sharing a link.
